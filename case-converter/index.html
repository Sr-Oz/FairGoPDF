Case Converter

Convert text between UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, camelCase and more. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

How it works

  1. Type or paste any text.
  2. Click a case style, the text updates in place.
  3. Copy the result.

Works out spaces from existing separators (spaces, hyphens, underscores) and from camelCase/PascalCase boundaries in your input, so you can convert between any of these styles, not just from plain text.

Proudly built for straightforward document management, 100% client-side, nothing is ever uploaded.
