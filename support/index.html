Shout a Mate a Coffee

FairGo PDF is built by one person, not a company, and it stays free because people like you chip in when they can.

Here's the honest version: FairGo PDF isn't backed by a big company, a board of investors, or a stack of ads tracking your every click. It's built, properly built, with actual care, by one independent developer who reckons your files are yours, full stop.

Every tool on this site runs right there in your browser. No uploads, no accounts, no sneaky "free" tier that's secretly selling your data. Just tools that work, the way they should've always worked.

But servers, a domain that doesn't lapse, and the hours it takes to build and maintain all this don't pay for themselves. So instead of plastering the site with ads or locking features behind a paywall, we're doing it the mate's way: if FairGo PDF has ever saved you a headache, and you've got a spare few bucks, we'd love it if you shouted us a coffee. No pressure, no guilt trip, just a genuine thanks in advance if you do.

Where it goes: every contribution goes straight back into keeping the lights on, hosting, the domain, and the time it takes to build new tools, squash bugs, and keep everything running smoothly. No investors to please, no shareholders to answer to. Just a mate, doing right by the tools you rely on.
