Contact form

The Contact Us page sends messages through Web3Forms, a third-party relay, so messages reach Fair Go PDF without an email address ever appearing anywhere in this site's source, where it could be scraped. Web3Forms sees the name, email and message you submit. It doesn't set cookies on this site, and the form sends a background request rather than redirecting you to their site. Using the contact form is entirely optional, and turning on Private mode (the shield at the top of every page) hides the form and stops this site contacting Web3Forms or hCaptcha at all.
