N-Up PDF

Combine multiple pages onto a single sheet, great for handouts, thumbnails and saving paper. Processed locally, nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  1. Add a PDF file.
  2. Choose how many pages to fit per sheet.
  3. Click "Create N-Up PDF" to download the combined result.

Each sheet keeps the size of your original pages, with the chosen number of pages shrunk down and arranged in a grid.
