How to make a free printable calendar PDF

Published 20 August 2026 · 3 min read

Most calendar templates online are either a locked-down image you can't write on, or a spreadsheet you have to fight with to print properly. A plain PDF, one page per month, with real empty space in each day, works better for pinning to a wall, printing for a planner, or loading onto a tablet to annotate.

Step by step

  1. Open Calendar Generator and set the year.
  2. Choose whether the week starts on Monday or Sunday, and A4 or Letter page size.
  3. Optionally list dates to mark, one per line, birthdays and anniversaries usually don't need a year attached.
  4. Click "Create Calendar PDF", you get 12 pages, one per month, ready to print.

A few practical tips

  • Two date formats, two different behaviours. 2026-12-25 Christmas only marks that exact date in that exact year. 12-25 Christmas marks December 25th every year you generate, useful for anything that repeats annually, birthdays especially.
  • Shaded weekend columns are on by default, they make the working week easier to scan at a glance, turn the checkbox off if you'd rather every day look identical.
  • Regenerating next year's calendar takes ten seconds. Keep your list of recurring dates saved somewhere, paste it back in and just change the year.
  • Want practice sheets instead of a calendar? Handwriting Worksheets uses the same "print and write on it" idea for cursive and print practice.
