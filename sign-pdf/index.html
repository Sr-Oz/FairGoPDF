      How it works

      1. Add a PDF. It opens on the last page, where signatures usually go. Use Previous and Next to move to another page.
      2. Draw your signature with a mouse or finger, type your name in a handwriting style, or upload a photo or scan of it. Pick the ink and pen. Tick "Keep this signature on this device" to use it again next time.
      3. Add it to the page, then add a date, your name or a tick where the form asks. Drag each one into place, resize it, and copy it to other pages, for initials on every page. Click "Sign PDF".

      This stamps your signature and any text onto the pages as part of the page. It is a visual signature, not a certified digital signature with an audit trail, so a document that needs one will need a dedicated e-signature service. Text with characters the PDF's own font lacks, such as macrons, is placed as a picture so it still shows. Saved signatures live only in this browser, and you can forget them at any time. Your PDF is read and changed in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.
