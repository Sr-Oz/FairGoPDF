How it works

  1. Add a PDF and pick which page to sign.
  2. Draw your signature with your mouse or finger, or switch to "Type" and enter your name.
  3. Choose where on the page it should sit and how big, then click "Sign PDF."

This stamps an image of your signature onto the page, it's a straightforward visual signature, not a cryptographically verified e-signature with an audit trail. For documents that require the latter, you'll need a dedicated e-signature service.

Proudly built for straightforward document management, 100% client-side, nothing is ever uploaded.
