Printable Calendar Generator

A 12-month calendar PDF, one page per month, with room to write. Mark birthdays, holidays or anything else. Processed locally.

One per line: YYYY-MM-DD Label for a specific year, or MM-DD Label to repeat every year, birthdays and anniversaries usually don't need a year.

How it works

  1. Set the year, which day the week starts on, and page size.
  2. Optionally list dates to mark, one per line, with or without a year.
  3. Click "Create Calendar PDF", you get one page per month, 12 pages in total.

Each day gets enough empty space to jot something down, print it, or open it in any PDF annotator, on a tablet, on a reMarkable, wherever you plan. Weekend columns are shaded lightly by default so the working week stands out, turn that off if you'd rather every day look the same.

Proudly built for straightforward document management, 100% client-side, nothing is ever uploaded.
