    How it works

    1. Add a PDF. It shows on the page, and the watermark shows on it as you change the settings.
    2. Type the text, or choose a picture. Set its size, colour, angle and opacity. Put it in one place or repeat it across the page, and choose which pages get it and whether it sits on top or behind.
    3. Click "Add watermark" to download the copy. Use Previous and Next to check other pages first: pages of a different size or turned sideways get the mark in the right place.

    A watermark is part of the page, so it can't be switched off afterwards, but a determined person can still cover or crop it, so it deters copying and marks a draft more than it protects. Text with letters outside the standard fonts, such as macrons, is stamped as a picture so it still shows. Several pages share one copy of a picture, so a logo on every page adds little to the file size. Your PDF is read and changed in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.
