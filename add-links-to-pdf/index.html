How it works

  1. Add a PDF and pick a page from the thumbnails.
  2. Click and drag on the preview to draw a link area.
  3. Choose whether it opens a website or jumps to another page in the same document, then save it.
  4. Repeat on any other pages, a dot marks pages with at least one link.
  5. Click "Add Links & Download."

The link area itself is invisible in the final PDF, just like a normal hyperlink in a web page, nothing is drawn on top of your content. Want visible text or a button graphic where people click? Add that first with PDF Editor, then draw the link area over it here.
