How it works

  1. Add two or more photos, then drag one onto another to swap their positions, or click "Shuffle" to randomise the order.
  2. Pick a layout (grid, rows, columns, feature, mosaic, frame or stack) and a canvas shape (square, story, A4 or a custom size).
  3. Adjust spacing and corner rounding to taste, and set a background colour.
  4. Click a photo, then pick a filter for it from the panel on the right, each photo can have its own, or use "Apply to all photos" to match them all at once.
  5. Add a caption if you want one, choose its position, size and colour.
  6. Click "Save & Download" to export the finished collage as one image.

Every photo fills its slot edge to edge (cropped to fit), rather than being placed and sized freely, "Feature," "Frame" and "Stack" pick which photo becomes the highlight based on order, so reorder first if you want a specific one front and centre. Need to place, resize and rotate photos entirely by hand instead, with no cropping? That's a different interaction model this tool doesn't cover.
