How it works

  1. Add a PDF file. If the page count isn't a multiple of 4, blank halves are added to pad it out.
  2. Click "Create Booklet PDF", you get back landscape sheets with two original pages side by side, arranged in booklet order.
  3. Print double-sided with "flip on short edge", then fold the whole stack in half and staple along the fold.

Each output page is one printed side, in front/back/front/back order. Your printer's duplex setting has to match "flip on short edge" (sometimes called "short-edge binding") for the backs to line up correctly after folding.

Proudly built for straightforward document management, 100% client-side, nothing is ever uploaded.
