How it works

  1. Add a PDF. The text is read straight away, and pages with no text to read are named, so you know when a file is a scan.
  2. Choose how to read it: tidy paragraphs in reading order, with columns read one at a time, headers and footers left out and split words joined, or exactly as laid out on the page, which keeps columns and forms lined up. Pick Markdown to keep headings, lists, tables, bold, italic and links.
  3. Pick the pages, search the text, then copy it or download it as a .txt or .md file. The text updates as you change a setting.

This reads the text a PDF already holds. A scanned page is a picture, so there is nothing to read until it goes through OCR PDF. Order is worked out from where the text sits, which is right for most documents; a complicated page with sidebars or overlapping boxes can still come out in the wrong order, and "As laid out on the page" is then the safer choice. Your PDF is read in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.
