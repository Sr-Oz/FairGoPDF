How it works

  1. Paste any piece of writing into the box above.
  2. Click "Check My Writing" and every match is flagged right there in your browser.
  3. Scroll down for a breakdown of what was found and why it's flagged.

This checks for wordlists and phrase patterns only, there's no AI model, no server call and no data leaves your browser. It flags the mechanical stuff (banned words, filler phrases, overused em dashes, cliché structures like "it's not X, it's Y"), it can't judge tone, rhythm or whether a metaphor lands, that still needs a human read. Rules adapted from the open-source no-ai-slop project by Peter Yang (MIT licensed).

Proudly built for straightforward document management, 100% client-side, nothing is ever uploaded.
