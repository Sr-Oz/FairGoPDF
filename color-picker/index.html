How it works

  1. Pick a color, type a HEX code, or drag the RGB/HSL sliders, they all stay in sync.
  2. Chrome and Edge also offer "Pick from screen", to sample any pixel on your screen.
  3. Copy whichever format you need.

Everything runs locally, no color you pick is ever transmitted anywhere.
